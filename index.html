Our journey began with a simple thought shared by a group of women—to create a space on campus where children could discover the joy of reading. What started with a handful of donated books has grown into a little haven of stories, learning, and creativity.
In a world increasingly dominated by digital screens, we aim to nurture a love for reading while focusing on the holistic development of children through engaging activities and meaningful experiences.
We believe childhood is the perfect time to nurture lifelong habits. Through our initiatives, we strive to instil values of sustainability, kindness, and responsibility towards the environment.
For us, this library is more than a collection of books; it is a small step towards nurturing curious minds, compassionate hearts, and responsible citizens of tomorrow.
Our little library continues to grow, bringing together 130+ members, both children and adults, and a collection of 700+ books and 60+ events conducted so far, fostering a shared love for reading across generations.
